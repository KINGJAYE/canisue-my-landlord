The Tenant Defense Playbook

Critical Dos and Don'ts While Your Landlord Is On Notice

01. Never Withhold Rent Informally (The Escrow Rule)

Do not simply stop paying rent into your pocket. Landlords wait for this mistake so they can file a fast non-payment eviction. If state law permits rent withholding for habitability failures, funds must be deposited into a dedicated, documented escrow account after the statutory notice period expires.

02. Keep Every Interaction in Writing

Once your certified notice lands, your landlord may call you to make verbal promises or pressure you. Follow up any phone conversation with a brief text or email: "Following up on our call today to confirm you stated the repair will happen on [Date]." Upload screenshots straight into your Vault Evidence Locker.

03. Document Any Sudden Retaliation

Both Pennsylvania and New Jersey laws prohibit landlord retaliation after a tenant exercises their legal housing rights. Log any sudden fee additions, lock threats, or hostile messages immediately inside your vault.
